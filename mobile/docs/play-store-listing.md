## Full description

Take working-platform calculations to site with Piling Mat Designer, an offline engineering calculation aid from Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd.

Enter project ground conditions, platform material properties and verified plant load cases. Explore cohesive and granular subgrades, inspect the calculation trace, and compare unreinforced and reinforced options.

Features:

- Offline calculations using the existing BR 470-based calculation engine.
- Clear inputs for ground properties, material properties and effective track loading.
- Saved project snapshots held on your device.
- Draft calculation records with a schematic cross-section and calculation steps.
- Print or save a draft PDF through Android's print service.
- No advertising, account login or subscription.

For competent temporary works designers and practitioners. Ground conditions, plant loads and material properties must be verified for each project. Results are draft calculation outputs, not construction approval, an independently checked design or a Working Platform Certificate. Project-specific engineering judgement and the applicable temporary works procedure remain essential.

The app purchase provides the software only. Formal consultancy, design checking and certification are not included. BR 470 refers to the published guidance; the app is not represented as endorsed or certified by BRE.

Saved projects remain on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Save important draft reports separately using Android printing.
