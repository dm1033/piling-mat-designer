Piling Mat Designer: Privacy Policy

Provider: Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd. Effective date: 30 October 2026.

Offline project data

This policy applies to the offline Android edition, version 0.1.0. The app has no internet permission, account login, analytics or advertising SDK. It does not transmit project names, calculation inputs or results to us. Saved project snapshots remain in the app's private local storage. Android backup and device transfer of this app's data are disabled.

Deletion and exported files

Delete individual snapshots within Saved projects. Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove all local projects. Printed or exported PDFs are saved or shared only through the Android print service you choose. Those copies and that service's processing are governed separately by your choices and the provider's policies.

Purchases and support

Google Play processes app download purchases under Google's policies. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your enquiry. Please do not send unnecessary personal data with a support request.

Contact

For privacy queries or support: david@temporaryworksconsulting.com.

Changes

This policy applies only to the described offline edition. We will update it if the app's data practices change.
